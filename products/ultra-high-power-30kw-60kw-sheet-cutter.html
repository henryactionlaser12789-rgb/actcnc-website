Core Engineering Advantages

Segmented Foundationless Bed

Modular 6m unit splice design separates the load-bearing cutting table from precision guide rails, preventing thermal deformation caused by burning thick plates.

Supersonic Laval Nozzle Flow

Accelerates auxiliary cutting gas to supersonic velocity (Mach 1.8), blowing away dense molten dross instantaneously and ensuring vertical bevel-free cut faces.

Intelligent Real-Time Slag Detection

Optical sensor feedback inside the laser head continuously monitors back-reflection and piercing progress, automatically adjusting laser frequency to prevent burst piercing.
