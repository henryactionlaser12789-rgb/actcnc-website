MOPA Color Fiber Laser Marking Machines (20W–100W)

Master of Color & Delicate Substrates: High-contrast pure black marking on anodized aluminum, vibrant rainbow colors on stainless steel, and zero-melt engraving on delicate plastics.

Engineered for High-End Marking

JPT / Raycus MOPA Fiber Engine

Unlike standard Q-switched lasers with fixed pulse widths, ACTCNC MOPA machines allow independent adjustment of pulse duration (2ns to 500ns) and frequency (1kHz to 4000kHz). This fine control prevents thermal burning while producing intense surface oxide coloration.

Applications: Consumer electronics logos, surgical tools, jewelry, automotive badges, and luxury hardware customization.
