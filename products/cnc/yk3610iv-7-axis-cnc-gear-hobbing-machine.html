Technological Advantages

Direct-Drive Torque Spindles

Both the hob spindle and the workpiece table eliminate traditional worm-gear transmissions, providing zero backlash and extreme angular synchronization for micro-precision gear profiles.

Eco-Friendly Dry Hobbing

Operates without cutting fluid using high-thermal-resistance carbide hobs, slashing coolant disposal costs, eliminating oil smoke, and keeping finished gear parts completely dry.

4-Second Automatic Loading

Integrated double-gripper CNC gantry loader achieves rapid 4-second part swap times, enabling single-operator supervision over a multi-machine cell.
