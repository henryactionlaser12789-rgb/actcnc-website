Key Structural Highlights

15-Second Rotary APC

Enables operators or automated robots to load/unload raw parts on pallet 1 while the machine is actively cutting on pallet 2, maximizing spindle utilization above 92%.

Heavy Hardened Box Guideways

Induction-hardened and precision-ground rectangular box guideways with Turcite-B anti-friction coating deliver extreme vibration damping during heavy steel and cast iron roughing.

2-Step Mechanical Gearbox

Integrated German ZF 2-step planetary gearhead provides massive low-end torque for large diameter boring and face milling without compromising high-speed finishing.
