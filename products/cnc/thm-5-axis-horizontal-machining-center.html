Core Engineering Advantages

Direct-Drive Torque Motors

The A/B rotary cradle axes feature zero-backlash direct-drive water-cooled torque motors, eliminating gear wear and ensuring dynamic acceleration for complex curved blade machining.

Thermal Symmetrical Meehanite Casting

Internally symmetric cooling jacket design channels constant-temperature cutting fluid through the casting core to isolate spindle motor heat, maintaining sub-micron accuracy over 24-hour continuous cuts.

FMS Turnkey Line Readiness

Seamlessly integrates with Ningjiang standard automated pallet pools (6-pallet, 12-pallet) and central MES scheduling software for 24/7 lights-out unmanned manufacturing.
