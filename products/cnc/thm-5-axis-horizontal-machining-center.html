THM Series 5-Axis Precision Horizontal Machining Center

Engineered for complex aerospace impellers, turbine blades, and precision automotive molds with simultaneous 5-axis contouring and high dynamic rigidity.

Core Structural Advantages

The THM series adopts a positive T-shaped integral bed, double-wall symmetrical thermal box column, and direct-drive A/B axis cradle rotary table to deliver exceptional machining accuracy and surface finishing.

Direct-Drive A/B Cradle

High-torque torque motors eliminate backlash for micro-arcsecond contouring.

Thermal Symmetry Bed

Meehanite dense cast iron with internal cooling channels for zero thermal drift.
