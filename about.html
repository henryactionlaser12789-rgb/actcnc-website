Our Engineering Core Pillars

📐

National Grade-1 Metrology Rigor

Equipped with Zeiss CMMs, Renishaw dynamic laser interferometers, and a 20±0.5°C constant-temperature precision spindle laboratory. Every machine undergoes full volumetric error compensation before dispatch.

⚙️

Meehanite Dense Cast Iron Bed

All machine beds utilize double-wall Meehanite casting with secondary thermal aging annealing (650°C for 24 hours), guaranteeing near-zero internal stress and 30+ years of geometric retention without deformation.

⚡

Dual-Engine Innovation (CNC + Laser)

Combining traditional high-torque mechanical milling centers with 30kW–60kW ground-rail fiber laser cutting technology, empowering shipyards, aerospace, and EV manufacturers with end-to-end processing power.

Milestones in Precision Machine Tool Manufacturing

Over six decades of state-level technical research, national science awards, and international export milestones.

1965 – 1980s

Foundation & National Metrology Base

Established as a key state-owned precision machine tool manufacturer. Awarded China National Grade-1 Measuring Enterprise status, supplying high-precision gear inspection instruments and jig borers for national defense and aerospace.

1990s – 2010s

Pioneering Chinese FMS & CNC Hobbers

Developed China's first commercial Flexible Manufacturing System (FMS) with automated RGV pallet transfer and host MES integration. Launched the benchmark YK3610 series high-speed CNC gear hobbing machines.

2010s – 2020s

Simultaneous 5-Axis Centers & Heavy HMC

Rolled out the THM series 5-axis horizontal machining center with direct-drive A/B cradle tables and the TH6363/TH63125 heavy dual-pallet HMC lines, capturing leading market share in automotive and aerospace.

2020s – Present

Global Expansion & Ultra-Power Laser Integration

Incorporating 30kW–60kW modular ground-rail laser plate cutting and 4-chuck zero-tailing tube laser systems into Ningjiang's global machinery export catalog, serving heavy fabricators across 50+ countries.
