Client Case • Baden-Württemberg, Germany

Automotive Tier-1 Hot Stamping: 3D 5-Axis Fiber Laser Trimming & CMM Quality Verification

Case Snapshot • Factory Performance Record Client: Leading German Tier-1 Automotive Chassis Manufacturer (Stuttgart / Göppingen region).
Application: 3D high-speed trimming and hole piercing of 1500MPa boron-alloyed hot-stamped steel B-pillars and bumper reinforcements.
Deployed Asset: ACTCNC Dual-Station 3D 5-Axis Robotic Fiber Laser Trimming Cell (4kW Fiber).
Key Result: Takt time reduced from 4.8 min to 1.8 min per part; die tooling maintenance costs slashed by €180,000 annually with 100% Zeiss CMM compliance.

1. Production Challenge: Die Wear on Usibor 1500 High-Strength Steel

Hot-stamped boron steel exhibits an ultimate tensile strength exceeding 1500 MPa. Traditional mechanical trimming dies suffered severe abrasive chipping and burr degradation after only 8,000 hits, requiring frequent line stoppages for die recoating and regrinding.
