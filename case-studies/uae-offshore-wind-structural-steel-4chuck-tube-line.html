Client Case • Abu Dhabi, UAE

Offshore Energy & Structural Truss: Heavy 4-Chuck 3D Tube Laser Line with Zero Tailing Waste

Case Snapshot • Factory Performance Record Client: Major Offshore Energy & EPC Contractor (Mussafah Industrial Zone, Abu Dhabi).
Application: 3D saddle coping, slotting, and ±45° multi-pass weld beveling on Ø219–Ø426mm heavy-wall steel pipes (Q355NH / API 5L Gr.B) up to 12 meters in length.
Deployed Asset: ACTCNC 12kW Heavy-Duty 4-Chuck 3D Tube Laser System with 12m Auto-Loader.
Key Result: Tailing scrap reduced from 1200mm to 0mm per tube; annual raw material savings exceeded $240,000 with 100% AWS D1.1 weld pass rate.

1. The Offshore Structural Challenge: Complex Saddle Intersections & Material Waste

Offshore jacket tubular nodes require complex intersecting saddle cuts with variable bevel angles ranging from 20° to 50°. Manual oxy-fuel cutting required 45 minutes of manual layout and heavy grinding per node, with severe risk of weld porosity under ultrasonic (UT) testing.
