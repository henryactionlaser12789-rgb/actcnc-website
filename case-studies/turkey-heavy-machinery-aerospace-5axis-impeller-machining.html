Client Case • Ankara / OSTIM, Turkey

Aerospace Turbomachinery: Simultaneous 5-Axis Precision Machining of Inconel 718 Centrifugal Impellers

Case Snapshot • Factory Performance Record Client: Leading Aviation & Gas Turbine Subcontractor (OSTIM Defense & Aviation Cluster, Ankara).
Application: 5-axis simultaneous roughing and aerodynamic finishing of closed Inconel 718 impellers (Φ380mm) and Ti-6Al-4V compressor blisks.
Deployed Asset: Ningjiang THM6380IV 5-Axis Precision HMC with Direct-Drive Cradle & 70-bar HPC.
Key Result: Aerodynamic blade surface roughness reached Ra 0.38 µm; overall machining cycle time per impeller reduced from 14.5 hours to 5.2 hours.

1. The Precision Challenge: Deep Narrow Ruled Blades & Tool Chatter

Machining complex twisted aerodynamic blades in nickel superalloys requires long tool overhangs. The client struggled with vibration chatter on their legacy 5-axis machines, resulting in poor blade surface roughness and excessive solid carbide tool breakage.
