30kW–60kW Fiber Laser Cutting in Heavy Structural Steel & Pressure Vessel Fabrication

As global shipyards, pressure vessel manufacturers, and bridge constructors transition away from traditional oxy-fuel and plasma cutting, extreme-power fiber lasers (30,000W to 60,000W) have revolutionized heavy fabrication productivity, reducing pre-weld preparation cycles by up to 75%.

1. The Mechanical Limits of Plasma vs. Ultra-High Power Laser

Oxy-fuel produces a wide Heat Affected Zone (HAZ) exceeding 3.5mm on 50mm carbon steel plates, necessitating expensive edge milling before certified submerged arc welding (SAW). ACTCNC's 60kW ground-rail laser limits HAZ to under 0.4mm while maintaining pristine verticality without slag accumulation.

2. Integrated ±45° Heavy Beveling System

Equipped with high-torque servo-driven 3D beveling cutting heads (Raytools / Precitec), the machine executes V, Y, K, and X bevels in a single continuous NC pass, directly feeding robotic welding cells without manual bevel prep.
