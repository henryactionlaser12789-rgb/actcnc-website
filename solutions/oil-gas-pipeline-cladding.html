Oil & Gas Pipeline Cladding, Valve Hardfacing & Drill Bit Remanufacturing

Extreme downhole environments require components to withstand high H2S, chloride stress corrosion, and severe abrasive wear. ACTCNC internal and external laser cladding systems deposit Inconel 625, Stellite 6, and Tungsten Carbide powders onto pipeline bores, BOP valve gates, and mining drill tools.

  • Deep Bore Cladding: Internal cladding heads reaching up to 6 meters inside drill pipes and cylinder bores.
