Laser Cladding & Direct Energy Deposition (DED) in Oilfield Valve & Tool Remanufacturing

In harsh subsea and sour gas (H2S / CO2) environments, drilling tools, mud motor rotors, and Christmas tree gate valves suffer severe abrasive wear and stress corrosion. Laser cladding provides dense, pore-free metallurgical coatings with dilution rates under 3%, extending critical component life by over 500% compared to traditional plasma transferred arc (PTA) welding and thermal spraying.

1. Metallurgical Dilution & Heat Input Comparison

PTA and submerged arc cladding generate massive heat input that causes deep dilution of the substrate into the corrosion-resistant alloy (CRA) layer, requiring 3 to 4 passes to achieve corrosion resistance. ACTCNC internal bore and external gantry laser cladding systems achieve virgin alloy chemistry in a single pass.

2. Deep Internal Bore Laser Cladding Capabilities

ACTCNC specialized internal bore cladding optical heads reach up to 6 meters depth inside pipes with internal diameters as small as Φ60mm, depositing wear-resistant nickel-based tungsten carbide matrix powders directly onto high-stress inner walls.
