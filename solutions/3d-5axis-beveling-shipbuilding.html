3D 5-Axis Robotic Laser Beveling in Modern Shipbuilding & Offshore Wind Fabrication

In mega-shipyards and offshore jacket foundation fabrication, curved hull plates and intersecting tubular joints require precision multi-pass weld preparation. ACTCNC 3D 5-axis robotic laser cutting cells replace manual plasma gouging and grinding with sub-millimeter automated beveling, slashing assembly fit-up time by up to 65%.

1. Precision Weld Preparation: V, Y, K, and Variable Beveling

Manual weld groove preparation frequently results in non-uniform root face gaps exceeding 3mm, causing excessive weld deposition and distortion. ACTCNC robotic systems maintain a ±0.2mm root face tolerance along continuous 3D curved surfaces.
