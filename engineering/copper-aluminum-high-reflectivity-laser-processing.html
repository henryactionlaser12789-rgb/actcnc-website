High-Reflectivity Copper & Aluminum Laser Welding and Cutting (EV Battery & Busbar)

Pure copper (C11000) reflects over 95% of standard 1064nm infrared laser wavelength at room temperature, posing severe risks of optical module burnout from back-reflection. ACTCNC integrates multi-stage anti-reflection isolators and oscillating wobble beam optics, achieving zero-spatter, defect-free welding for EV battery modules, power distribution busbars, and copper heat sinks.

1. Optical Absorption Dynamics: Cold State vs. Molten Keyhole

Once copper transitions to the molten state, laser absorption spikes from 5% to over 65%. High-frequency dynamic wobble heads agitate the melt pool, preventing premature keyhole collapse and eliminating gas entrapment blowholes.

2. Anti-Reflection Hardware & Fiber Source Protection

Dual-stage optical isolators monitor backward light leakage in real-time (<0.2ms response time), shutting down excitation if dangerous back-reflection exceeds threshold values, safeguarding laser diodes for over 100,000 operational hours.
