Supersonic Laval Nozzle Dynamics & Boundary Layer Slag Ejection

In ultra-high power laser cutting (20kW–60kW) of thick structural plates (30mm–80mm), standard conical nozzles produce normal shock waves at the plate entrance, dissipating dynamic gas pressure and allowing molten slag to re-adhere to the lower kerf. ACTCNC de Laval convergent-divergent supersonic nozzles accelerate assist gas to Mach 1.5–2.2, maintaining parallel high-momentum jet streams through deep kerf slots.
