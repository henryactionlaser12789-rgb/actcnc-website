💡 Direct Engineering Answer: How to Optimize Fiber Laser Cutting Speed & Cost?

Fiber laser cutting performance depends on the balance of laser power density, assist gas purity/pressure, and nozzle orifice selection. For Carbon Steel (>12mm), High-Pressure Oxygen (O2 at 0.5–1.2 bar) generates an exothermic reaction for clean vertical cut faces. For Stainless Steel and Aluminum, High-Pressure Nitrogen (N2 at 14–22 bar) or High-Pressure Dry Air (16–20 bar) prevents oxidation, yielding slag-free edges ready for direct robotic welding without secondary grinding.
