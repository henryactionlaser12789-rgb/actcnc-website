Fiber Laser Operating Cost Breakdown: Electricity, Gas & Consumables

Understanding your hourly cost per meter is critical to quoting parts competitively while preserving profit margins. Below is a real-world operating cost model for a 12kW fiber laser cutting 6mm carbon steel and 3mm stainless steel.

1. Electrical Power

Laser source + water chiller + CNC motion system consumes ~28 kWh on a 12kW system ($0.12/kWh = $3.36 / hour).

2. Assist Gas Consumption

Oxygen for carbon steel ($0.80/hr) vs Liquid Nitrogen ($12.00/hr) vs High-Pressure Compressed Air ($1.50 / hour).

3. Consumable Wear

Protective windows, copper nozzles, ceramic rings, and focal lens replacement averages $0.60 / hour.
