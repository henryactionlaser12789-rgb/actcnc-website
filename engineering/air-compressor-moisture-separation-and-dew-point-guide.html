Pneumatic Purity Standards

Air Compressor Moisture Separation & Pressure Dew Point Monitoring Guide for High-Power Lasers

Using compressed air as an assist gas saves up to 75% in operating costs, but oil aerosols and moisture micro-droplets are fatal to optical protective windows under high-power (10kW–60kW) irradiation. A single micron of residual oil vapor absorbs 1064nm photons, causing instantaneous thermal runaway and shattered cover slides.

2. Inline Dew Point Sensor Integration & Automatic Laser Interlock

ACTCNC gas control skids feature digital inline ceramic capacitive dew point sensors. If moisture rises above -20°C (indicating dryer failure), the system triggers an emergency beam interlock before optical surfaces are damaged.
