Pneumatic Servo Engineering Whitepaper

High-Pressure Proportional Valve Response & Corner Anti-Burn Pressure Modulation

When high-power fiber lasers decelerate into acute corners (<90°), excessive heat builds up in the localized melt pool. If assist gas pressure cannot be stepped down within milliseconds, violent uncontrolled oxidation results in severe corner overburn and rounded melt edges. ACTCNC ultra-fast proportional servo valves achieve a 0–25 Bar step response in under 15ms, synchronizing gas pressure with CNC motion acceleration in real time.
