Laser Cutting & Welding Process Defect Resolution Matrix

An engineer's diagnostic guide to identifying and fixing dross/slag, thermal burnouts, cut edge discoloration, and porosity in real-time shop floor operations.

Observed Defect / Symptom Root Cause Analysis Immediate Operator Solution Recommended Parameter Adjustment
Bottom Dross / Slag on Stainless Steel Insufficient N2 gas pressure or focal point positioned too high above sheet. Increase Nitrogen assist pressure (16–20 Bar) and lower the focal position into the bottom 1/3 of plate thickness. Focal Position: -2.0mm to -4.0mm
Gas Pressure: +3.0 Bar
Corner Burnout / Overheating on Carbon Steel Excessive heat buildup at sharp vertices during oxygen cutting. Enable CNC Corner Deceleration and Power Frequency Modulation (Curve Power Control). Enable "Corner Cooling Point"
Reduce Duty Cycle to 60% at turns
Rough Striations / Vertical Grooving Cutting speed too high or dirty/damaged protective window absorbing beam energy. Inspect lower protective lens under 100X microscope. If clean, reduce feed rate by 15%. Feed Rate: -10% to -15%
Check Nozzle Centering & Coaxiality
Piercing Blowout on Extra-Thick Plates (20mm+) Single-stage blast piercing causing explosive molten spatter backfire. Switch from direct piercing to 3-Stage Progressive Frequency-Ramped Piercing with Oil Spray. Use Stage 1-3 Pulse Piercing
Nozzle Stand-off: 5.0mm → 1.0mm
