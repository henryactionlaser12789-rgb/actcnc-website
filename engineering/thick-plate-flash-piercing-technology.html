Thick Plate Flash Piercing Dynamics & Supersonic Laval Nozzle Ejection in 30kW–60kW Laser Systems

Published by ACTCNC Heavy Machinery R&D Center | Target: Shipbuilding, Pressure Vessels & Heavy Crane Fabricators

1. The Physics of Heavy Plate Piercing (20mm–100mm)

In traditional multi-kilowatt fiber laser cutting, piercing carbon steel plates above 30mm thickness often encounters explosive back-spatter and optical thermal lensing. ACTCNC's progressive frequency ramping algorithm modulates laser pulse frequency (50Hz to 5000Hz) synchronously with millisecond pneumatic proportional valve pressure stepping.

⚡ Experimental Piercing Benchmark (Q235B Carbon Steel, 50mm Thickness)

  • Conventional 3-Stage Piercing: 8.5 seconds cycle time, 120mm spatter radius, high nozzle contamination risk.
  • ACTCNC Flash Piercing (60kW): 1.15 seconds cycle time, zero slag accumulation, 86% cycle speedup.

2. Tube Beveling Mathematical Modeling (±45° Saddle Curve)

When cutting intersecting saddle lines on large-diameter pipes (Φ520mm) for structural trusses and offshore platforms, the spatial angle between the laser nozzle axis and pipe tangent plane changes continuously. Our 4-chuck numerical control system calculates real-time tool center point (TCP) kinematics to maintain a constant focal distance of 0.2mm throughout the entire 360° rotation.

3. Economic Impact & ROI Calculation

By eliminating manual bevel grinding and secondary edge milling, a structural steel fabricator processing 200 tons of heavy pipe per month saves an average of $38,000 in consumable grinding discs and skilled labor costs annually.
