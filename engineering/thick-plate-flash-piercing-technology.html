30kW–60kW Heavy Plate Flash Piercing & Anti-Burst Technology

Piercing thick structural carbon steel (30mm to 100mm) is the single highest-risk phase in ultra-high power laser cutting. Traditional multi-stage ramp piercing generates massive upward molten slag ejections that foul optical cover slides and trigger catastrophic nozzle blow-ups. ACTCNC's intelligent variable-frequency flash piercing algorithm slashes piercing time by over 90% while achieving complete spatter containment.

2. Dynamic Optical Focus & Gas Pressure Stepping Control

By synchronizing real-time peak laser pulse power modulation with millisecond-level proportional gas valve staging, the molten crater expands radially downward rather than exploding upward, extending lower cover slide lifetime from 4 hours to over 180 hours.
