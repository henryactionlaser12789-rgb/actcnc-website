Photonics Thermal Engineering

Thermal Focus Shift & Optical Lensing Real-Time Compensation (30kW–60kW)

Under continuous heavy plate cutting (>30kW power), even fused silica optical elements with 99.9% transmission absorb fractional energy, establishing internal thermal gradients (thermal lensing). This causes the actual focal point to drift upward by 2.0mm to 4.5mm within 15 minutes of continuous operation, causing severe kerf bottom burrs and sudden loss of cut penetration.
